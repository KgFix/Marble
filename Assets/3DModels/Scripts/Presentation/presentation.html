📷 Dynamic Camera System

Camera Features

  • Procedural Following: Smooth camera tracking of marble
  • Dynamic Tilt: Camera tilts based on movement direction (±20°)
  • Speed-Based Adjustment: Tilt interpolation at 5 units/sec
  • Input Responsive: Only tilts when player has control
